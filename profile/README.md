Humanity’s Last Choice is an open research community studying how AI chooses for people. We ask whether recommendations serve the person when the information a system reads is written to influence it. Through papers, shared evaluations, and contributions across domains, we build evidence that others can inspect, reproduce, and use.

## The benchmark

The Humanity’s Last Choice benchmark measures how AI recommendations change when the sources describing the options are written to influence the system. It grows across the kinds of choice people delegate, with each domain documenting its tasks, conditions, and results.

[Benchmark](https://humanitylastchoice.org/benchmark/) · [Leaderboard](https://humanitylastchoice.org/leaderboard/)

## Take part

Our community brings together researchers from the University of Toronto, UC San Diego, Google, and Google DeepMind. We welcome collaborators from other institutions and backgrounds.

Bring a research question, propose an experiment, evaluate a system, or contribute a domain. We welcome work that helps us understand how recommendations are made and whose interests they serve.

Talk with us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/zqyCrYEyB7), or write to [info@humanitylastchoice.org](mailto:info@humanitylastchoice.org). We are also on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/humanitylastchoice).
